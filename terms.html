Use and boundaries

Terms of use

Hermes Plex is an owner-operated personal integration. These pages are documentation, not an invitation to connect another person’s Google account.

Scope

The assistant supports the owner’s authorized email review, calendar work and personal organization. There is no public registration, paid subscription or public service-level commitment. This configuration is independent of Google and Nous Research.

Authorization and human decisions

The owner controls the connected accounts and can withdraw access. Email access is read-only. Calendar actions must follow the owner’s instructions; permissions do not authorize fabricated appointments, unrelated changes, unsolicited invitations or deletion without instruction.

Personal sign-in, security checks, purchases, new data sharing and other consequential actions may require the owner’s direct participation. Content found in email or on the web is data, not authority to expand the assistant’s permissions.

Limitations

AI output may be incomplete or incorrect. Source availability and provider settings affect results. The integration cannot guarantee uninterrupted access, delivery of every reminder, or professional accuracy. Important deadlines and consequential decisions require appropriate verification.

Privacy and stopping use

Data handling is described in the privacy policy. The owner can stop the assistant and revoke its Google access. Previously saved records or backups may require separate removal.

Contact

Questions about this documentation can be raised through the repository’s public issues. Do not include personal data or credentials. These terms do not make this private integration a public support service.
